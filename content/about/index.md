# About Me

I'm a software architect who's very passionate about software development and the build systems that hold them together. I care a lot about how code is structured — not just technically, but in terms of readability and how well it separates business logic from the technical plumbing underneath. That intersection between language design and day-to-day developer experience is something I find endlessly interesting.
